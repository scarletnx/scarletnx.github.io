ScarletNX / ARM ISA

ARM ISA

The instructions ScarletNX recompiles, laid out as Arm's reference lays them out and made from the Sail it recompiles them from: sail-arm's model of Armv9.4-A, and ScarletNX's fixups of it. Each instruction has its encodings, their decode and its operation; every name they use is a page of the shared pseudocode.

  • A64: the AArch64 instruction set, as a Switch program runs it (303 instructions, 332 encodings)
  • A32 and T32: the AArch32 instruction sets, from the ARM7TDMI to the Cortex-A57 (109 instructions, 149 encodings)

The search takes a name (the nearest when none is the same) or an instruction word in hex (0x1a020020, or a Thumb halfword 0x4148): it lists every encoding it matches.

The GPUs' instructions

  • Maxwell ISA: Maxwell (SM53) (154 instructions, 266 encodings)
  • Ampere ISA: Ampere (SM86) (178 instructions, 419 encodings)
