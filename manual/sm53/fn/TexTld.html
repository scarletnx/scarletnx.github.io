sm53_texture.sail

SM53's texture and surface instructions (sm53_texture.roots; back end sm53_texture_spirv.h). Each is the translator's binding of the textures and images to the pipeline as much as an operation: the slot the prescan found for its handle (bound, bindless or an indexed table's element), the typed slots and their element swaps, the scaled render targets, the depth comparison by the slot's function, the min and max filters, the offsets' dispatch trees, the coordinates' registers by the texture's kind. That is emit_texture.c's (shader_tex_*), as SM86's texture() and surface() are sm86_translate.c's, which each instruction runs
