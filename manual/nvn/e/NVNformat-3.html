ScarletNX / NVN API

NVNformat

nvn.h

The Vulkan format whose texels are laid out as the NVN format's, as ScarletNX's tables give it (nvn_tic_format.inc, then vk/format.c); a block-compressed format the GPU can't sample, ScarletNX decodes. The link is the format's definition on docs.vulkan.org.
