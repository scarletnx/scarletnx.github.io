ISETP and UISETP: a and b compared (76-78, signed with 73), combined (74) with the predicate at 87 into the one at 81, and its negation into the one at 84. No negation: bit 72, a's negation in the decoder's fields, is .EX (NAK's OpISetP, whose sources take no modifiers). .EX: equal high words take the low words' comparison, the predicate at 68

let op = Field(76, 3);
var cmp : bool = IntCompare(op, Field(73, 1) == 0b1, SrcBits(A), SrcBits(B));
if Field(72, 1) == 0b1 then {
    let low = PredAt(68);
    let equal = SrcBits(A) == SrcBits(B);
    if op != 0b000 & op != 0b111 then { cmp = (if equal then low else cmp) }
};
let acc = PredAt(87);
let set_op = Field(74, 2);
PredWrite(UInt(Field(81, 3)), Combine(set_op, cmp, acc), Uniform());
PredWrite(UInt(Field(84, 3)), Combine(set_op, not_bool(cmp), acc), Uniform())
