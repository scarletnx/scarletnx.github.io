TEX, TLD, TLD4, TXD and TXQ with the handle in a uniform register: the constant buffer codes with bit 10, the register at 40-45 in place of the constant buffer's reference, bit 91 set, the rest of the fields theirs. CuAssembler's sm_86 records: "TEX.SCR.LL.DC R34, R8, R2, R6, UR4, 2D" (0x00000000099e4f08_3000040602227f60) and "TXQ R35, R20, UR12, TEX_HEADER_DIMENSION, 0x2" (0x00000000080002ff_00000c0014237f6f). One game's programs take the handle from memory: an LDG.64 of a table of handles, R2UR, then UR + 1 = UR >> 20, the sampler's index. Another game's fragment and compute programs sample with TXD this way (0xf6c, with TXD's fields: Lichii's lowering of it)
