Ampere ISA: SM86

The SM86 instructions (Volta to Ada's 128-bit encoding, Ampere's) that ScarletNX's shader translator takes, laid out as Arm's reference lays an instruction out: each encoding's diagram (its opcode from sm86.decode, the fields the decoder and the Sail read) and the operation in ScarletNX's Sail. 178 instructions, 419 encodings; 136 with Sail.

The search takes a name (the nearest when none is the same) or a 128-bit instruction in hex, its high 64 bits first (0x000fe20007ffe0ff_0000000000007210), or its low 64 bits: its opcode is bits 15:0.
