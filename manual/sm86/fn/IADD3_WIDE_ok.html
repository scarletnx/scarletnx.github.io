IADD3_WIDE_ok

sm86.sail

IADD3 lo, P, a, b, RZ then IADD3.X hi, a', b', RZ, P, !PT: the 64-bit sum (a':a) + (b':b), or with b and b' negated the difference, as NVIDIA's compiler adds a 64-bit offset to an address. Each instruction's sum is made in 64 bits for its carries; the pair is one 32-bit add, its carry, and the high words' add, with the same results: lo, the carry in P, hi. The low word's write must leave the high word's sources as they were

function IADD3_WIDE_ok() -> bool = {
    let p = UInt(FieldAt(0, 81, 3));
    FieldAt(0, 74, 1) == 0b0 & p < PT & UInt(FieldAt(0, 84, 3)) == PT & IsZeroAt(0, C) & not_bool(SrcNegAt(0, C))
    & FieldAt(1, 74, 1) == 0b1 & UInt(FieldAt(1, 87, 3)) == p & FieldAt(1, 90, 1) == 0b0
    & FieldAt(1, 77, 4) == 0xf & UInt(FieldAt(1, 81, 3)) == PT & UInt(FieldAt(1, 84, 3)) == PT
    & IsZeroAt(1, C) & not_bool(SrcNegAt(1, C))
    & not_bool(SrcNegAt(0, A)) & not_bool(SrcNegAt(1, A)) & SrcNegAt(0, B) == SrcNegAt(1, B)
    & not_bool(ReadsDstAt(1, A, 0)) & not_bool(ReadsDstAt(1, B, 0))
}
