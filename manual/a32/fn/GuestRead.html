GuestRead

ScarletNX's fixup arm.sail

---- guest memory ---- Guest memory is the board's own (llvm.c's readmem and writemem: flat, checked or hosted, with the ARM7TDMI's and the ARM946E-S's bus rules and hook). GuestRead and GuestWrite are one access of size bytes; aligned is MemA's and MemS's (the multiple transfers', whose alignment the ARMv4T and ARMv5TE buses do not check) against MemU's, which takes any address

val GuestRead : forall 'size. (bits(32), int('size), bool) -> bits('size * 8)
