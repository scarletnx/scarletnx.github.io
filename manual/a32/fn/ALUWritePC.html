ALUWritePC

ScarletNX's fixup arm.sail

A data processing write of the PC in A32 interworks from ARMv7 on; ARMv4T to ARMv6 branch in A32, to the word its bits 1 and 0 cleared leave (the ARMv7 ARM's ALUWritePC); a Thumb one branches in Thumb

val ALUWritePC : bits(32) -> unit

function ALUWritePC address = if CurrentInstrSet() == InstrSet_A32 & ArchVersion() >= 7 then
                                  BXWritePC(address, BranchType_INDIR)
                              else BranchWritePC(address, BranchType_INDIR)
