GuestTransferEmpty

ScarletNX's fixup arm.sail

An empty list (UNPREDICTABLE) ends an LDM or an STM UNDEFINED, as the block compilers always had it: the board whose core does something of its own with one runs it in its interpreter (the GBA's ARM7TDMI transfers R15 and moves the base by 0x40, agb/cpu/cpu.c). An empty PUSH or POP transfers nothing, its address checked, but on checked memory, whose bus has no transaction of no bytes (GuestTransferEmpty(), the back end's)

val GuestTransferEmpty : unit -> bool
