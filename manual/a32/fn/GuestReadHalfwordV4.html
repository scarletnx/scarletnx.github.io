GuestReadHalfwordV4

ScarletNX's fixup arm.sail

The ARM7TDMI's halfword loads at an odd address (ARMv4's UNPREDICTABLE) are its bus's: LDRH reads the aligned halfword rotated a byte through its 32 bits, LDRSH the byte there sign extended, which the GBA's games and tests read so. GuestReadHalfwordV4(address, signed) is that load's 32 bits; these are sail-arm's three T16 halfword loads with it in the place of ZeroExtend or SignExtend of MemU on ArchVersion() 4, the rest as they are

val GuestReadHalfwordV4 : (bits(32), bool) -> bits(32)

Used by

  • LDRH (register)
  • LDRSH (register)
