ITSTATE

ScarletNX's fixup arm.sail

---- the condition and the IT block ---- The block compilers decide an IT block's conditions when they compile it (llvm_thumb.inc): ITSTATE before each instruction is known when the recompiler runs, the back end's (ITSTATE), as is CurrentCond (sail-arm's AArch32_CurrentCond, a val its runtime answers): an A32 word's condition, the T1 and T3 B<c>'s own, else the IT block's or AL. ConditionPassed is then sail-arm's, the run-time test of the flags that sailgen runs the rest of the instruction under (sg_when)

val ITSTATE : unit -> bits(8)
