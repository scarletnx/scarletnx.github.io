HaveLSE2Ext

ScarletNX's fixup nx.sail

A pair of registers is one access of both (the pairs' path for FEAT_LSE2, which reaches HaveLSE2Ext alone here): GuestMemRead and GuestMemWrite make it one 64-bit access for a pair of words and two 64-bit ones for a pair of doublewords, the accesses ScarletNX always made. The NX's CPUs have no FEAT_LSE2 (a64.profile), whose 16-byte single-copy atomicity that second one is not. LDPSW and STNP, which Arm makes two accesses on any CPU, stay two
